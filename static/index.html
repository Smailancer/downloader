Questions

Which sites can I download from?

TikTok, Instagram (Reels and posts), Facebook, X (Twitter), YouTube, Reddit, Pinterest, Vimeo and many more. Private videos and posts that need a login can't be downloaded.

How do I download a TikTok or Instagram video on my phone?

Copy the video link from the Share menu, paste it here and tap Get video. If you install Downloader, it appears in the Share menu itself, so you skip the copy and paste.

Can I save only the audio?

Yes. Pick “Audio (MP3)” instead of a video quality.

Do you keep the videos?

No. Each file is deleted from the server about 20 minutes after it's ready.

Download only videos you own or have permission to save.
